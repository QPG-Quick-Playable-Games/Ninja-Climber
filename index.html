How To Play

Tap the left or right button (or press Space) to move your ninja between the two walls. Collect coins (+30) and power-ups, avoid spikes. Survive longer to earn periodic score bonuses.

  • Coins: +30 score
  • Power-ups: multiplier, extra life, slow spikes, score boost
  • Spikes: avoid — hitting one loses a life
